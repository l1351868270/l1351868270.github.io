## GSPO <d-cite key="group-sequence-policy-optimization"></d-cite>

$$
\begin{aligned}

\mathcal{J}_{GSPO} \left( \theta \right) &= \mathbb{E}_{x \sim \mathcal{D}, \{y_{i}\}_{i=1}^{G} \sim \pi_{\theta_{old}} \left( \cdot | x \right)} \\

&= \left[ \frac{1}{G} \sum_{i=1}^{G} \text{min} \left( s_{i}\left( \theta \right) \hat{A_{i}}, \text{clip} \left(s_{i}\left( \theta \right), 1-\epsilon, 1 + \epsilon \right)\hat{A_{i}} \right) \right]

\end{aligned}
$$
